PeriodT Coach helps coaches and support staff train female athletes with their cycle, not against it. See how each athlete is feeling, read the notes they choose to share, and adjust upcoming sessions in a few taps. Built alongside the PeriodT athlete app, it keeps the conversation about menstrual health and wellbeing open, private and kind.

Features & Functionalities

  • Team overview: search your staff and athletes and open anyone's profile.
  • Team readiness: a colour-coded grid shows each athlete's readiness score for the week.
  • Cycle-aware profiles: see an athlete's current phase, monthly readiness calendar and latest check-in.
  • Notes and journal: read what athletes choose to share and flag sessions that need a change.
  • Upcoming sessions: swap exercises or set Low, Moderate or High intensity for strength, conditioning and physio work.
  • Exercise library: videos, background and coaching cues, with sets and reps for each intensity.
  • Female Library: plain-language guides on period pain, heavy periods, PCOS, FHA and training through the cycle.
  • Individual goals: track progress toward each athlete's goals.

System Extensions

Notifications

Get told when an athlete sends you a note, and tap it to open their profile straight away.

Home Screen Widgets

Small and large widgets show new and unopened notes from your athletes, so nothing slips past before training.

Dark Mode

Every screen has a matching dark design that follows your iPhone's appearance.

How to Use

  1. Sign in with the coach account your club set up for you.
  2. Open Team Summary to see reminders, announcements and how ready your squad is this week.
  3. Tap an athlete to see their cycle phase, readiness calendar and the notes they've shared.
  4. Open an upcoming session to change an exercise's intensity or swap it for another.
  5. Use the Exercise and Female Library tabs to plan sessions and learn how to support each athlete.
